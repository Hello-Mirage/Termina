How it works

  1. Train

    We teach you the tech you need to build fast and ship a working project under pressure.

Can’t find skilled teammates for your next hackathon?

A community ready to build with you

You have a strong project idea, but finding reliable, skilled partners shouldn't hold you back. terminaLABS connects you with an active community of motivated developers and designers who are eager to team up, contribute to your vision, and build under pressure.

Lock your project idea securely on GitHub

Never worry about anyone stealing your concept. You can securely lock your project idea in a private GitHub repository that only you and your approved teammates can see. Your intellectual property stays 100% confidential and 100% yours.

An incubator with no money involved

There are no fees and no equity. We teach the tech. Every team owns its idea from start to finish.

  • Free to join and free to learn.
  • Your team brings the idea. We never take it.
  • Need a teammate for the next hackathon? Ask someone here.
  • We train small groups so everyone gets real attention.

We don’t go to participate.

Showing up is the easy part. We train, plan, and build to win, and we want teammates who feel the same.
